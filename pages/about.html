Who we are

Bridging technical possibilities with commercial reality

Fisdel Green Consult Ltd is a development-driven consulting company focused on structuring and advancing sustainable projects across energy, electric mobility, and green infrastructure. We help clients transform ideas into practical, well-packaged, and commercially sensible opportunities capable of attracting stakeholder confidence and implementation momentum.

Professional support at the intersection of sustainability and structure

We serve clients across residential, commercial, industrial, mobility, and infrastructure-related sectors. Our role is to help define project concepts, clarify business logic, improve bankability, and support strategic decision-making.

  • Project origination and structuring
  • Technical-commercial pathway design
  • Investor and lender presentation support
  • Implementation-oriented development support
